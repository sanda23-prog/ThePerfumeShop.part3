Our story

About The Perfume Shop

Established in 2023, The Perfume Shop is a beauty and personal-care retailer focused on making distinctive fragrances easier to discover and buy.

Our Mission

To offer a carefully selected range of fragrances at accessible prices while giving customers a simple, helpful and enjoyable shopping experience.

Why Choose Us?

  • Curated fragrance and home-fragrance selection
  • Clear product information and pricing
  • Delivery and Durban collection options
  • Product enquiries before purchase
  • Responsive customer support

Our Customer Promise

We aim to keep the experience straightforward from browsing and searching to adding a product, checking totals and receiving a printable demo receipt.
